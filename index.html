Institution Overview

About NMIT Bengaluru

Nitte Meenakshi Institute of Technology (NMIT) is an autonomous engineering college located in Yelahanka, Bengaluru. The institution provides undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral degrees approved by AICTE and affiliated to VTU and Nitte University.
